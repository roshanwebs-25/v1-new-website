Company Overview

A Hyderabad beginning in 2018, built on a clear vision.

V1 Facility and Security Services Private Limited commenced operations in Hyderabad in 2018 with a vision to achieve the highest standards for all integrated services. We are an ISO 9001 & OHSAS 18001:2015 certified company dedicated to providing reliable services with a team of experts from all the multifaceted segments.

Our integrated FM services include Security, Housekeeping, M&E, Pest Control and Gardening. With detailed documented Standard Operating Procedures and adherence to all statutory compliances, we serve IT MNCs, banks, hospitals and construction projects, among others.

Mr. Shreekar ReddyFounder & Chief Executive Officer
CEO Profile
Raising service standards and driving positive change in the industry.

After a decade of international exposure and global travel, Mr. Shreekar Reddy returned to India with a clear vision — to build a professional organization that creates meaningful employment and delivers world-class facility services. This vision led to the establishment of V1 Facility and Security Services Private Limited in Hyderabad in 2018.

Drawing on his global experience, he focused on empowering unskilled and semi-skilled professionals by creating structured job opportunities in India's growing facility management sector. Today the company operates across 8 states with 3,000+ trained professionals and 100+ reputed organizations, with a strong commitment to growth, quality and excellence.
