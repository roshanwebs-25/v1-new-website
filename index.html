About Us

Safer, Cleaner & Better Managed Facilities.

V1 Facility and Security Services Private Limited was founded in Hyderabad in 2018 by Mr. Shreekar Reddy, with a vision to build a professional organization that creates meaningful employment and delivers world-class facility services. Within four years it became a trusted integrated facility management partner for leading corporates and industries.

With 3,000+ trained professionals, an ISO 9001 & OHSAS 18001:2015 certification, a PSARA licence and a partnership with the National Skills Development Corporation (NSDC), we serve 100+ reputed organizations — including L&T and Marriott — across 8 states.
